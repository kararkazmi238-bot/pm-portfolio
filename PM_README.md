## Overview

A full end-to-end project management case study built around a 20-week EHR activation for a fictional 380-person hospital. This project demonstrates core PM and implementation competencies across all phases of a healthcare IT go-live — from workstream planning and training coordination to issue escalation, go-live gate management, and readiness scoring.

The scenario: serving as project manager for "Riverside Medical Center's" EHR activation, coordinating 6 workstreams across clinical, revenue cycle, IT, training, data migration, and command center functions simultaneously.

## Key PM Skills Demonstrated

- Multi-workstream project coordination (6 parallel workstreams, 214 tasks)
- Go-live readiness assessment and gate management
- Risk identification and escalation to steering committee
- End-user training program management (role-based, 8 tracks, 380 users)
- Issue tracking with SLA measurement (2.4-day avg resolution vs 3-day SLA)
- Data migration oversight and mock cutover management
- Change management and super-user deployment strategy
- Stakeholder communication and sign-off coordination
- Command center planning and hypercare support design

## Why This Matters for Healthcare IT Roles

Healthcare IT implementation roles — at companies like Epic, Oracle Health, Veeva, Accenture, Deloitte, and LeanTaaS — look for candidates who understand:

- **Go-live lifecycle**: discovery → build → UAT → training → cutover → hypercare
- **EHR workflow terminology**: order sets, charge capture, downtime procedures, elbow support, mock cutovers
- **Risk management in clinical settings**: HIPAA compliance, data validation, patient safety considerations
- **Training coordination at scale**: role-based curricula, super-user programs, makeup session management
- **Issue escalation**: tiered priority, cross-functional ownership, SLA tracking

This project speaks directly to all of the above — without claiming experience I don't have.
